# CSI 300: China's S&P 500 — Top 50 Analysis Candidates

## 1. What is the Chinese equivalent of the S&P 500?

The **CSI 300 (沪深300)** is China's equivalent of the S&P 500. It tracks the 300 largest and most liquid A-share companies listed on both the Shanghai and Shenzhen stock exchanges. Key facts:

- Launched April 8, 2005, base value 1,000
- Free-float capitalization-weighted
- Semi-annual rebalancing (June & December)
- Compiled by China Securities Index Company

## 4. Analysis Approach

Use the **Pulak Prasad framework** (from `skills/pulak-prasad-investing-from-darwin-en.md`) + **InvestSkill prompts** for each candidate:

```
[1] Kill List check → Any exclusion criteria triggered? → REJECT
[2] ROCE consistently >15%? (5+ years) → No? → REJECT
[3] Moat source clear and sustainable? → Unclear? → REJECT
[4] Management integrity + competence? → Doubt? → REJECT
[5] Valuation reasonable? → Too expensive? → WATCHLIST
[6] BUY. Then be very lazy.
```

### Immediate Exclusions (likely Kill List failures for Chinese market):
- **SOEs** (Criterion #7): PetroChina, Sinopec, ICBC, ABC, Bank of China, China Mobile, China Unicom, China Telecom, China State Construction, CRRC, etc.
- **High leverage**: Banks (all), Insurance
- **Rapidly changing industry**: BOE Technology (display cycles), LONGi (solar oversupply)
