## Executive Summary

Lam Research is a top-tier Darwin-quality business: ~52% ROCE, 65% ROE, 31% net margin, a fortress balance sheet (D/E 0.30, **no goodwill**), strong FCF, and disciplined capital return ($3.85B buybacks + $1.27B dividends). It is the clear quality leader of the semicap-equipment screen. The stock has dropped ~37% from its high and is **oversold (RSI 30)** on a macro/rate-driven dip, not a fundamental one — FY2026 was a record ($23.2B revenue) and Q1 FY27 guidance accelerates to ~$8.1B revenue / $2.00-2.30 EPS. **The one thing holding back a "buy now" call is valuation**: even after the drop, Graham IV implies no margin of safety (fair value ~$119-161 vs $272 price), because LRCX fell *from a bubble* and still trades at ~52x trailing / 33x forward. Verdict: **high-conviction quality, but initiate small and scale in on further weakness — the business passes, the price does not yet offer a Graham margin of safety.**

## 3. Moat Analysis

- **Technology + process moat**: #1/#2 in etch and deposition — mission-critical steps where process know-how and installed-base lock-in create high switching costs. Co-development with leading-edge fabs is deeply sticky.

- **AI tailwind**: HBM/DRAM and advanced logic (the AI supercycle) are etch/deposition-intensive; management says the order book shows the supercycle "isn't over."
- Durability: **Strong**, with cyclicality overlaid.
