## Executive Summary

NVIDIA is an exceptionally profitable business: FY26 revenue $215.9B, 60% operating margin, ~$97B free cash flow, net cash, and ~$40B of buybacks. On the numbers it is a top-tier financial-health pass. It is a **weaker Darwin fit than LRCX**, because two kill-list items are major cautions rather than minor ones: it operates in the fastest-changing industry there is (#4), and its revenue depends on a small number of very large customers and one product family (#8).

Valuation is mixed. It looks cheap on forward earnings (10.5x FY28 consensus EPS) and fair-to-rich on Graham and a scenario DCF. The base-case DCF is ~$188 and the Graham range is ~$157–$213, so $166 is **~11% below a central value of ~$185**. Your own rule for quality names that fail a strict screen requires **more than 25% below intrinsic value**, which implies ≤ ~$139.

**Verdict: quality business, major structural cautions, price does not yet offer your required margin of safety. Watchlist with written entry prices, no position now. Do not act ahead of the 11-17 earnings print.**

## 3. Moat Analysis

- **Software and ecosystem**: CUDA and its libraries create switching costs for developers and customers.
- **Full-stack integration**: GPUs, networking and systems sold together. This is a real advantage over single-component competitors.
- **Scale**: R&D was $18.5B in FY26, more than most competitors' total revenue.
- **Threats**: custom silicon from the hyperscalers themselves (the same customers who buy NVDA), AMD, export controls and China, and customers trying to cut a ~75% gross margin supplier out of their cost structure.
- Durability: **Strong today, the least certain part of the thesis.** The 75% gross margin is the number competition would attack first.

## 4. Management & Insider Activity

- Capital allocation: strong. Large buybacks, a rising dividend, light capex.
- Insider activity: **not pulled this run.** Check the DB/Form 4 data before any decision.

Net cash, ~$97B FCF, current liquidity ample, light capex. Passes the solvency test cleanly. Watch the rise in debt and the doubling of inventory.

## 6. Valuation

**Multiples:** trailing PE 21, forward PE 10.5 (on FY28 EPS $15.85), price/sales ~13x TTM, FCF yield ~2.4% on FY26 FCF, earnings yield ~4.8% TTM.

**The forward multiple is a statement about doubt.** 10.5x on FY28 consensus means the market does not expect that EPS to arrive or last. If FY28 EPS comes in at about half of consensus (~$8), the stock sits at the current trailing PE of ~21.

**Graham IV** (Y = 6.30% AAA, the October 2026 figure from the LRCX analysis, not refreshed):

|---|---:|---:|---:|

| FY27E $9.32 | 15% | $251 | +51% |

Graham's growth input should be a 7-10 year figure. 10-15% is defensible for a company coming off +65% to +90% years, and 20% is not.

**Scenario DCF** (10-year FCF, terminal value; net cash $23.6B; 24.1B shares):

| Scenario | FY28 revenue | FCF margin | Discount | Terminal g | Value/share |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Bear | $520B | 45% fading to 30% | 12% | 3% | **~$99** |
| Base | $600B | 45% fading to 35% | 10% | 3% | **~$188** |
| Bull | $692B (consensus) | 48% fading to 40% | 9% | 3.5% | **~$318** |

Base-case sensitivity (value/share): 8% discount $205–$296 | 10% $149–$208 | 12% $117–$159 | 14% $96–$128 (across 25%–40% terminal FCF margin). Terminal value is 41%–62% of the total.

**Reverse DCF:** at a 10% discount rate, $166 requires only ~$526B of FY28 revenue, against $692B in consensus.

**Discount rate caveat:** beta is 2.25. CAPM at that beta implies ~14% or more, which gives ~$118 in the base case. The choice between 10% and 14% moves the answer by about 60%.

**Central intrinsic value used here: ~$185** (Graham TTM g=10–15% midpoint ~$185, DCF base $188, FY27E Graham g=10% $186).

**Graham verdict: marginal.** $166 is ~10% below the central value. Not a Graham margin of safety, not a bubble multiple either.

**Graham + Darwin synthesis:** Darwin passes with two major cautions, Graham is marginal. Under your rule (buy a quality name that fails the strict screen only if >25% below IV), the trigger is **≤ ~$139**.
