- LinkedIn ($26B, 2016): Revenue grown from $3B → ~$18B. Success.

**Organic vs. Acquired growth test**: FY2026 (ending Mar 31) revenue = $318B. So roughly **60% organic, 40% acquisition-driven** for the most recent year.

**Kill List #3 revised verdict**: ⚠️ Technically triggers, but PASSES with caveat. Darwin rejects acquirers because most destroy value. MSFT's track record shows value creation, not destruction. However, this is an exception, not a rule. If MSFT makes another $50B+ deal, reassess.
